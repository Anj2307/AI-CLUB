Manifesto

We are students who teach machines to see, to listen, to speak and to reason. Every model we train is a mirror — it shows us what we understand about intelligence, and everything we don't. We build things that are hardly human, so we can learn what being human really means. // and we ship.

— Hardly Human, IIT Dharwad
