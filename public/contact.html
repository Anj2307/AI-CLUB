FAQ

Frequently asked

Who can join Hardly Human?

Any student of IIT Dharwad - any branch, any year. No prior AI experience is required.

How do recruitments work?

We open applications at the start of each semester. Fill the form, attend a short interaction and pick the team you want to work with.

Do I need a powerful laptop?

No. Members get access to club compute and cloud credits for project work.

Can external organisations collaborate with us?

Yes - we welcome industry talks, workshops, hackathon sponsorships and research collaborations. Drop us an email.
